RoKu's URL Sanitizer

Paste a TikTok or YouTube link. You get back a plain link with the tracking removed, including the share IDs that tell the app who sent it. Nothing is downloaded, logged, or stored.

Clean link

    TikTok: full links and short links (vm.tiktok.com, vt.tiktok.com, tiktok.com/t/). Short links are expanded by following the redirect only; the video itself is never fetched.

    YouTube: videos, youtu.be, Shorts, live, embeds, playlists, channels, clips, posts and YouTube Music. Start times are kept; everything else (si, is, pp, feature, playlist info...) is removed.
