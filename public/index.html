RoKu's TikTok URL Sanitizer

Paste a TikTok share link (including short links like vm.tiktok.com or vt.tiktok.com). We resolve it to its canonical URL and strip every tracking parameter — nothing is downloaded, logged, or stored.

Clean link
Works with full share links (www.tiktok.com/@user/video/…) and short links (vm.tiktok.com/…, vt.tiktok.com/…, tiktok.com/t/…). Short links are expanded by following the redirect only — the video itself is never fetched.
